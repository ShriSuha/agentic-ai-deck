# opus-mt-en-de

## What it does

`opus-mt-en-de` translates written text from English into German. You give it an English sentence and it returns a German sentence. It does not answer general questions or describe images.

## Training data

It was trained on public parallel sentences from the OPUS collection, paired English and German text.

## Limits

- It translates in one direction only: English to German.
- It is a sentence translator, not a document writer.
- Names, slang, and rare words are often wrong.
- It was not trained to refuse unsafe requests.

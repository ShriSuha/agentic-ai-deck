# Llama-2-7b

## What it does

`Llama-2-7b` generates text. It is the 7-billion-parameter base model from the Llama 2 family. A separate chat variant was tuned for dialogue. This card is about the base model.

## License

Llama 2 Community License. Use is allowed with conditions, including extra limits once a service has a very large number of monthly users. It is not Apache 2.0 and it is not MIT.

## Training data

Publicly documented as a mix of publicly available text. Meta does not publish the exact file list.

## Limits

- The license is custom. Read it before shipping a product.
- The model card from the publisher warns about bias and about unsafe or untrue outputs.
- It does not know facts that appeared after its training cutoff.
- It does not classify images.

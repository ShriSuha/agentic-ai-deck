# gpt2

## What it does

`gpt2` continues a piece of English text. You give it a prompt and it predicts the next words. It is a general text generator, not a translator and not an image model.

## License

MIT License.

## Training data

Web pages collected in OpenAI's WebText dataset.

## Limits

- It often invents facts. Nothing it writes is checked against a source.
- Training text includes harmful and biased material, and the model can repeat those biases.
- It has no separate "I don't know" behavior.
- It does not look at images.

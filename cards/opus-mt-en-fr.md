# opus-mt-en-fr

## What it does

`opus-mt-en-fr` translates written text from English into French. The input is an English sentence. The output is a French sentence.

## Training data

Paired English and French sentences from the OPUS collection.

## Limits

- One direction only: English to French.
- Long documents must be split into sentences first.
- It can miss tone and idiom.
- It does not work with images or speech.

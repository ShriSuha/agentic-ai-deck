# nllb-200-distilled-600M

## What it does

`nllb-200-distilled-600M` translates written text among more than 200 languages. "Distilled" means this is a smaller, faster copy of a larger translation model. It is meant for text translation, including languages that have little training data.

## Training data

Parallel text collected for the No Language Left Behind project.

## Limits

- The license does not allow commercial use.
- Translation quality still varies widely by language.
- This distilled size is less accurate than the largest NLLB models.
- It does not read images.

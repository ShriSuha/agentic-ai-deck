# wav2vec2-base-960h

## What it does

`wav2vec2-base-960h` transcribes English speech into text. The audio goes in and words come out. It is not a text translation model and it is not an image model.

## License

Apache License 2.0.

## Training data

960 hours of the LibriSpeech audiobook recordings.

## Limits

- English speech only.
- Audiobook-style recordings work better than noisy meetings.
- It does not translate English text into another language.
- It cannot open or read a Markdown model card.

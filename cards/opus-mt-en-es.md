# opus-mt-en-es

## What it does

`opus-mt-en-es` translates written text from English into Spanish.

## License

Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY-4.0).

## Training data

Paired English and Spanish sentences from the OPUS collection.

## Limits

- One direction only: English to Spanish.
- It is weak on regional varieties of Spanish.
- It does not look at pictures or audio.
- Errors rise when the English sentence is very long.
